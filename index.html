SUDOKU

Heist

Crack nine vaults. Every number is loot.

The job

Sudoku Heist is a roguelike puzzle built on classic sudoku rules. Each run is three jobs of three vaults. Boards grow from 4×4 to 6×6 to 9×9, and every vault has a loot target you must reach before the board is full.

Solving is only half the game. The order you fill cells decides your score.

How scoring works

A correct digit earns 10 plus its value. Completing a row, column or box multiplies that placement:

1 line ×22 lines ×43 lines ×8

Save the cells that finish several lines at once, then cash them in for a big haul.

Stealth

Clean placements in a row build Stealth, which multiplies everything. A wrong digit costs a heart and resets Stealth. Lose every heart and the run is over.

Reading the board

Under the loot bar you'll see how many cells are left, how much loot you still need, and whether you're On Pace, Tight or Falling Short.

Gadgets

Between vaults, spend your cut at the Fence on gadgets that bend the scoring. You can carry four, and sell one back for half its price.

Box Cutter
Completing a box is ×3.

Tripwire
Completing a row or column is ×3.

Soft Shoes
Stealth rises twice as fast.

Final Turn
The last cell of a vault scores ×10.

Safety Net
Your first mistake in each vault is free.

Crowbar
Vault targets are 20% lower.

…and eight more to discover.

Security systems

The last vault of every job has a security system that changes the rules:

Laser Grid

Pencil notes are disabled.

Silent Alarm

The vault locks when the timer runs out.

Pressure Plates

Mistakes cost two hearts.

Cold Digits

Digits 1–3 earn no base loot.

Questions

Is every puzzle solvable without guessing?

Yes. Every board is generated on your iPhone and checked to have exactly one solution.

Can I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button on the title screen. Sound also follows your iPhone's silent switch.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Sudoku Heist works fully offline.

Can I see the rules again?

Tap How It Works on the title screen. The first vault also shows tips while you play.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Sudoku Heist does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is sent to any server. The app does not use the internet.
  • Your best score, tutorial progress and sound setting are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete. If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page.
